## Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Given the declared retention bound is at most 3 retained generations and a minimum retirement age of 24 hours
And the backend refuses the request that lists generation records
When the retention policy is evaluated
Then the evaluation reports that it could not assess the generation population, naming the refusal it received
And it does not report 0 generation records assessed and 0 reclaimable
And the could-not-assess result is distinguishable in the report from a result that assessed every generation record and found none reclaimable

Given the backend lists 12 generation records but refuses the request that counts entity nodes per generation
When the retention policy is evaluated
Then all 12 generation records still receive a reclaimability verdict
And the entity-node counts are reported as undetermined, naming the refusal
And the undetermined node counts are not reported as 0
And the evaluation states which part of its assessment succeeded and which part it could not complete

Given the backend has latched read-only
When the retention policy is evaluated
Then the evaluation reports the read-only condition as the reason it is constrained
And it states that reclaiming space is itself blocked while the backend refuses changes
And the read-only condition is not reported as an absence of generations or as an empty result
```
