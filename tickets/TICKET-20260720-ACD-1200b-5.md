## Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Given a goal AC with two leaf children at readiness: reviewed
  (for example ACD-050a-2-i and ACD-050b-1),
When a supported approval mechanism is invoked against that goal (a new
  approval script under scripts/ac_store/, for example approve_acs.py, and/or
  an --approve flag on the goal-to-epic command),
Then each of those reviewed leaf ACs has its readiness field promoted from
  reviewed to approved in its stored YAML,
And the promotion is performed by the mechanism, not by any hand-edit of the
  YAML files,
And the change is append-only with respect to history: an amended_by entry is
  added recording the promotion, and no other field of the AC is altered.

Given the same two leaf ACs after a first successful promotion run,
When the approval mechanism is invoked a second time against the same goal,
Then the already-approved leaves remain approved and are not duplicated,
  re-stamped, or otherwise modified (the operation is idempotent).
```
