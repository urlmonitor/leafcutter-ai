## Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Given the declared retention bound is at most 3 retained generations and a minimum retirement age of 24 hours
And the repository holds active generation G32 and retired generations G31 down to G1
And G29 was retired 2 hours ago
And an unexpired pinned continuation is held against G28
When the retention policy is evaluated
Then the evaluation reports the declared bound as an inspectable value naming both its generation-count limit of 3 and its minimum retirement age of 24 hours
And it reports the configuration source the bound was read from
And G32 is reported not reclaimable because it is the active generation
And G31 and G30 are reported not reclaimable because they are inside the declared count bound
And G29 is reported not reclaimable because it is younger than the declared minimum retirement age
And G28 is reported not reclaimable because its pinned continuation has not expired, even though it is outside both bounds
And exactly G27 down to G1 are reported reclaimable
And the evaluation leaves every generation, the active pointer and every continuation unchanged

Given no retention bound is declared in configuration
When the retention policy is evaluated
Then the evaluation refuses and names the unset retention bound setting
And it does not resolve the bound from a built-in value
And no generation is reported reclaimable

Given a declared bound names a generation count but no minimum retirement age
When the retention policy is evaluated
Then the evaluation refuses and names the missing minimum retirement age
And it does not treat generation count alone as the whole bound
```
