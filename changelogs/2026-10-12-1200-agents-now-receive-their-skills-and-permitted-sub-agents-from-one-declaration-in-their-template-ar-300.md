description: "AR-300 (branch feature/ar-300-skills-delivery, origin/main..HEAD, 11 commits). Agent templates declare skills (always or conditional, with a condition) and permitted sub-agents (spawn_allowlist, including the __ticket_phase_agents__ entry) in their frontmatter, and the build appends one generated Your Available Skills section and one Your Available Sub-Agents section to each compiled prompt. Before this, 0 of 60 agents received their skills because the injection depended on a marker no template contained. The registry fields skills_used, skills_invoked and spawn_allowlist are now derived copies regenerated by the new Transform agent registry pre-commit hook; spawned_by stays hand-edited. New build gates: per-agent preparation delivery; grant delivery, where a short, extra or unexpected section refuses; unresolvable skill or sub-agent ids and malformed declarations refuse; the Skill or Agent tool is required for a non-empty grant. Hand-written skills or sub-agent tables and the retired {{my_skills_used}} and {{my_spawn_allowlist}} markers are reported. 36 agents gained the Skill tool, build-ac and business-analyst gained Agent, most ticket-phase signoff grants became conditional, and python-coder declares complexity-reduction. architect-review-deep and conflict-resolver-deep were removed because they were never built (KI-AR-004 tracks the follow-up); conflict-resolver now resolves structural conflicts itself and pull-request shows the diff for structural resolutions. Docs: the agent-template-frontmatter reference and the creating-an-agent-template and deprecating-or-removing-artifacts how-tos were updated. KI-AR-003 is resolved and KI-AR-005 (project-local agent lookup) is filed."
