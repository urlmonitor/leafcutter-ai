## Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Given a project record holding a known number of artifacts of each type,
When the record's checker runs,
Then its report states, for each check it performed, how many records that check read,
And adding one further artifact of a type a check reads raises that check's stated figure by

And no stated figure is carried over from an earlier run.
```
