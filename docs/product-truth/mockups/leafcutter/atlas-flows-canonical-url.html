Canonical, shareable flow URLs

Every flow lives at /flows/<flow id>, and every step or branch at /flows/<flow id>?step=<step id>. Opening, selecting or drilling updates the address bar; back and forward follow the selection; an unknown flow id shows a 404 notice and an unknown step id a "Step not found" notice. The bare /flows address is the finder (screen atlas-flows-finder). Flow-page quick wins are added at the end: actor badges, Enter and the named close button, filter changes that keep the drawn flow, and a tab title per flow.
