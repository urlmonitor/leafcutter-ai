## Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Given a ticket whose record names a phase as needed and carries no sign-off for it,
  And every other phase that record names as needed does carry one,
When a close of that ticket is attempted,
Then the finished state is not written,
  And the ticket's recorded state is left exactly as it was found,
  And the attempt is refused with a message naming the phase that is unaccounted for,
  And the ticket's record still names that phase as needed afterwards -- the refusal is
    not resolved by rewriting the demand away, and the demand is not reclassified as
    not-needed, excluded, or already satisfied as a side effect of being refused,
And given a second ticket carried by the same run, whose record names the same phases as
  needed and carries a sign-off for every one of them,
When its close is attempted,
Then the finished state is written for that second ticket,
So the refusal is a decision taken about the evidence in the record, and not a mechanism
  that has simply stopped writing; and the only way to turn the first ticket into the
  second is to account for the outstanding phase, never to make the check more agreeable.
```
