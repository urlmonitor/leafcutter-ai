## Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Given a generated ticket that requires documentation and carries the
  `### documentation-expert` Agent Contracts block,
When `documentation-expert` runs, it reads that same block as its Contract-Aware brief;
  and when `documentation-verifier` runs, it asserts against that same block's

  produce and what the verifier checks, keeping brief and enforcement in lockstep.
```
