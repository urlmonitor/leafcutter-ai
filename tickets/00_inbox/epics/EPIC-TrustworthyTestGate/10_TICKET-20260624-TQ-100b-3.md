## Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Given the enforcement behavior defined by TQ-100b-1 (informational while the
  covering AC is not done) and TQ-100b-2 (enforced once the covering AC is
  done),
When a state diagram is authored for a tagged test's enforcement lifecycle,
Then the diagram shows the states a tagged test can occupy (at least:
  informational/non-blocking, enforced/blocking, and unlinked-enforced)
And it shows the transitions between them and what triggers each transition
  (the covering AC's work_status reaching done, and a tag resolving to no AC)
And a maintainer can read from the diagram exactly when a test starts blocking
  the run.
```
