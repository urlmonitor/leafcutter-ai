## Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Given a person deciding where to put a new artifact, or a person auditing what is example
  content and what is not,
When they open the reference on example content,
Then it states the rule that decides which product an artifact belongs to, and that the rule
  is the product root rather than the artifact's content,
And it lists every surface that honours the separation - the project's record, the store of
  work, and the reading surfaces - and states what each returns for example content,
And it states that the example content is required to keep existing, naming the decisions that
  depend on it, so a future reader does not read the separation as a plan to delete it,
And it states how to add a new example product and how to add a new example artifact to an
  existing one.
```
