# The consistency check catches a wired relationship present on one side (card or registry) but not the other

## Actor / Goal

As the leafcutter-ai system, I want to implement AC `INF-600l-1` — The consistency check catches a wired relationship present on one side (card or registry) but not the other — so that the acceptance criterion is satisfied.

## Context

This ticket was generated from AC store entry `INF-600l-1`. Component: `infrastructure`. Assigned agent: `python-coder`. Estimated complexity: `M`.

## Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Given the existing check_agent_spawn_consistency check is extended to compare an
  agent's generated card against its registry entry
And agent "python-coder"'s generated card states it can spawn "sql-coder"
  (the card shows a spawn edge python-coder -> sql-coder)
But "python-coder"'s registry entry does not list "sql-coder" in the corresponding
  spawn relationship (the registry has no such edge)
When the build runs the consistency check
Then the check reports a card<->registry mirror mismatch that names both agents and
  both sides, e.g. "python-coder card shows spawn edge to sql-coder, but the
  registry has no such edge"
And in the reverse case -- the registry has a wired relationship the card does not
  show -- the check reports the mismatch the same way, naming the side that is
  missing the edge
And when the card and the registry agree on the wired relationships, the check
  passes with no mismatch reported
```
