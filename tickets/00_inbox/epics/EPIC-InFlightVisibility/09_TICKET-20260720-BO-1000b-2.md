## Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Given each finalize step has recorded a one-line outcome as it ran,
When the finalize run reaches its end and produces its final summary,
Then the end-of-run summary is composed from the recorded per-step outcomes,
  enumerating each step alongside the specific outcome it recorded,
And the summary is not a single bare overall status such as "done" or an
  "ok" acknowledgement with no per-step detail.
```
