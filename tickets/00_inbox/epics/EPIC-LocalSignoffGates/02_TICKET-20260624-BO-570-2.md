## Context

## Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Given a ticket whose changed surface includes one or more frontend files
  (e.g. a route/page or component the user can see),
  and frontend-coder has finished its implementation work,
When frontend-coder evaluates its sign-off for the ticket,
Then it actually renders the changed frontend surface locally
  (a dev/route render of the affected page, not a static or lint check alone),
And a render-time-only defect — such as a component configured with
  server-side rendering disabled that only throws at route render time —
  is detected during this local render,
And on a render failure the sign-off is recorded as failed (status: failed),
  with the render failure surfaced as the blocking reason,
And frontend-coder does NOT sign off "ok" while the changed surface fails
  to render.

Given the changed frontend surface renders successfully end to end
  when exercised locally,
When frontend-coder evaluates its sign-off,
Then the dev-render smoke check passes,
And the sign-off may proceed to "ok" on this criterion,
And the render-time-only defect class is caught before the change reaches
  commit, the PR, or CI.
```
