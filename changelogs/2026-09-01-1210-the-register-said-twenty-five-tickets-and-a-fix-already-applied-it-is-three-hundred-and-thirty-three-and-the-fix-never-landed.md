description: "One entry recorded that every ticket in an epic carried a defect that halts a build at the finish line, gave the figure as twenty-five, and said all twenty-five had been corrected by hand. Counting the actual tree gives three hundred and thirty-three affected tickets across twenty-three bodies of work, three hundred and sixteen of them unfinished, and the commit that applied the hand correction is reachable from exactly one branch which has not landed. So on the mainline none of them is corrected. Both errors point the same way — they make the problem look smaller and further along than it is — and either would lead someone to plan a small repair for something that needs a large one. The entry now carries the measured figures with the command that produced them, keeps the reasoning behind the hand correction because that reasoning is sound and worth preserving, and scopes it plainly to the branch it was made on. A warning has been added that follows only from the real numbers: the check that ought to stop these tickets currently has two routes into it and only one of them looks at the ticket, so roughly two in three slip past into a false finish rather than halting. That leniency is the only reason a population this size has been survivable. Making the check consistent — which is being specified now, and is correct — turns that into every one of the three hundred and sixteen halting reliably. The repair of the existing population therefore has to run first, or the next build stops on the first of them with three hundred and fifteen behind it. The second entry's stated fix direction is right but was read in opposite senses by two different readers on the same day, so the reasoning is now written down rather than left to be re-derived: taken alone the direction makes the blocker permanent instead of intermittent, which is why it looks wrong; the alternative that looks obvious — letting the check simply believe the caller about which steps do not count — must be rejected, because a check that accepts the account of the party it is checking has stopped checking; and the direction only completes the fix when paired with making the record itself true, so neither half works alone."
