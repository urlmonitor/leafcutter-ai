## Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Given the feature-planning workflow has finished the technical planner stage for a batch
  of 4 leaf code requirements,
When the workflow continues,
Then the code-aware test-design step runs on those 4 requirements before the final
  approval gate is shown,

  approval.
```
