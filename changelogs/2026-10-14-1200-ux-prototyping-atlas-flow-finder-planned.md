description: "Planned with the owner deciding at each gate after a UX review found the Flows view hard to use. Bare /flows becomes a finder overview: one search over flow names, step text, input and output field names, criteria, entities and screens; filters with counts for product, actor (Jev as its own value), kind, realization, level, fields used and produced, entity, screen and steps whose inputs or outputs are not defined yet; results grouped by flow that open at the matching step; and the search kept in the /flows address so a link reproduces it. Quick wins on flow pages: a filter change never swaps the flow you are viewing, step cards show who runs them, steps open and close from the keyboard, each flow page has its own tab title, and criterion chips open that criterion. The mock data (atlas-flows v4), a new finder mockup, the updated canonical-url mockup and the explore-flows-in-atlas flow (v6) were approved; UXP-617 and 14 sub-criteria were added, and six earlier UXP-591 criteria that assumed bare /flows opened a default flow were rewritten. No application code yet: building the criteria is the next step."
