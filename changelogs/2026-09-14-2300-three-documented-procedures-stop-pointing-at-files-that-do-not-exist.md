It is derived from the two existing siblings and from reading nine existing how-tos rather than invented, and it records two competing step-heading shapes that are both in live use instead of retroactively mandating one, and calls the type: how_to spelling a legacy divergence from the canonical type: how-to rather than pretending both are valid. Second, docs/reference/ held 22 documents and no index while reference-author.md step 5 and write-reference.md step 9 both instruct the author to update one, making that step a no-op on every run since the surface was created; the new README describes each page from its content rather than its frontmatter, which matters because six of the pages have no usable description field and so appear in the generated docs/INDEX.md as a bare title, and it states its relationship to docs/INDEX.md so neither is later deleted as redundant with the other. Third, docs/reference/artifact-numbering.md is the page docs/architecture/diagrams/README.md deferred to with the words 'once that document exists'; it was built from check_diagram_naming.py's regex, next_diagram_seq.py, check_identifier_uniqueness.py and its scanners, the CI numbering job and ADR-029, cross-checked against 34 GE-122 records, and that deferral line is corrected here. The numbering page records three divergences rather than silently resolving them: two different filename regexes exist for the same convention, since the commit-time hook requires a strict c<level>-<3 digits>-<slug> shape on staged files carrying type: architecture while the uniqueness scanner uses a looser case-insensitive pattern over every .md regardless of type, so a file can be visible to one gate and invisible to the other; eleven files in docs/architecture/diagrams/ do not match the enforced pattern and are dormant only because the hook is staged-files-only, none renamed here since that is a restructure; and GE-122's own L0 promises that a clash surfaces at a point where it cannot be skipped, which is not true today because the commit-time stage is bypassable and GE-122d-2, which would make the CI job a required check, is still todo. GE-122a-3, GE-122b-4 and GE-122d-5 are approved-but-unbuilt ACs specifying a fuller version of the numbering page, so it says so in its lead and a future documentation-expert extends it rather than creating a duplicate. Deliberately untouched because they are owned by INF-1300 records merged earlier the same day and being built concurrently: the ADR-015 legend attribution, the unrunnable --files verification command, the omitted description checklist entry and the --date disagreement (INF-1300b-4); docs/architecture/README.md (INF-1300c-2); and the draft-indexing contradiction (INF-1300e-2)."
