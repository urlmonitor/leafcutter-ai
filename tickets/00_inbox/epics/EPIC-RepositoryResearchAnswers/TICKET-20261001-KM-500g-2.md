## Implementation Notes

```yaml
- Preserve immutable source provenance, bounded registered read-only queries and existing
  authorization, budgets and retries. No arbitrary Cypher, new network endpoint, source-write
  permission, independent telemetry store or automatic activation is added.
- Join diagnostics only by matching request/attempt and source/generation. Keep current
  workspace, requested/published source and historically tested revision as separate
  optional values.
- Record evidenced failure class, supporting diagnostics, known expected/observed
  repository/revision/query/permission scope and governing contract locator. Missing
  comparison values stay unknown and cannot come from another attempt.
- Distinguish required-field/projection/disclosure gaps, retrieval outages and zero
  matches. Credentials/network/provider/transformation causes need matching specific
  diagnostics; absent or conflicting diagnostics produce unresolved/unknown cause.
- Next actions identify a bounded case and why it addresses the limitation. No proven-minimality
  claim, automatic publication/rollback, expanded permission or new data transfer
  follows from diagnosis.
- RQE-06-P/N source-oracle expectations judge actual responses but cannot supply diagnostic
  evidence missing from those responses.
```
