## Entry

The knowledge-routing step, which runs the harvester at the end of a completed unit of
work, now has documentation for both kinds of reader.

- **How-to**: `docs/how-to/confirm-or-route-a-captured-learning.md`. Steps to confirm a
  completion path routed a learning, to run the step by hand, and to read the
  `--status` answer. It treats `config/guardrail_gates.yaml` (`knowledge_routing_wiring`)
  as the authority for which paths are wired.
- **Reference**: `docs/reference/knowledge-routing-step.md`. The completion paths, the
  three routing outcomes (completed, could not complete, did not run), the `--status`
  fields and the manual run.
- Index rows in `docs/reference/README.md` and `docs/INDEX.md`, plus a pointer from
  `docs/architecture/agent_knowledge_system.md`.
- **AC store**: INF-700a-3 and INF-700a-4 are done. INF-700a stays open.
