## Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Given an L2 leaf code requirement whose criteria carry 3 outcome clauses (Then/And):
  (1) the check reports that 4 records were checked,
  (2) the overall result is failed,
  (3) the report heading uses the project's house wording,
When the technical planner enriches it,
Then the record carries one test-intent entry per outcome clause, 3 in total,
And each entry names its clause, states needs-test yes or no, and gives a one-sentence
  reason,
And the record carries no test design written by the planner: no test names, target
  directories, frameworks, test types or test angles,
And the criteria text is byte-identical before and after enrichment,
And for a requirement that produces no executable behaviour (a prompt- or docs-only
  change) the planner may instead record that no tests are required, as today.
```
