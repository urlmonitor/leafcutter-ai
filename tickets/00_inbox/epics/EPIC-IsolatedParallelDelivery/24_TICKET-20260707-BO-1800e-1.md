## Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Given a drive running in its own isolated clone,
When the drive performs any of its steps (scaffold, implementation, finalize
  bookkeeping),
Then none of those steps commit onto a local `main` branch,
And the clone's `main` is treated as a read-only tracking branch of the hub -- all
  of the drive's own work happens on a feature branch, never on `main`.
```
