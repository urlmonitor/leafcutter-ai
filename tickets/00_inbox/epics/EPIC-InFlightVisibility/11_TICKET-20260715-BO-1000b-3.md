# Step outcomes and the recap carry concrete result data, never a content-free 'done'

## Actor / Goal

As the leafcutter-ai system, I want to implement AC `BO-1000b-3` — Step outcomes and the recap carry concrete result data, never a content-free 'done' — so that the acceptance criterion is satisfied.

## Context

This ticket was generated from AC store entry `BO-1000b-3`. Component: `build-orchestration`. Assigned agent: `python-coder`. Estimated complexity: `S`. Complexity: `low`.

## Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Given a finalize step performed work that produced measurable results (counts,
  identifiers, or decisions such as tickets closed, source ACs closed and
  skipped, pre-existing failure counts, the pull request number, or the merge
  result),
When that step's outcome line and the final recap are emitted,
Then the outcome text states the concrete result data for the step rather than
  a content-free acknowledgement,
And a step that did real work never reports its outcome as only "ok" or "done"
  without the accompanying result data.
```
