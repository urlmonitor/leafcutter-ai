## Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Given the declared retention bound is at most 3 retained generations and a minimum retirement age of 24 hours
And generation G5 records a failed publication, was created 40 hours ago, and carries no retirement time because it was never retired
And generation G6 records a failed publication, was created 2 hours ago, and carries no retirement time
When the retention policy is evaluated
Then the evaluation states, for each generation carrying no retirement time, which recorded time its age was measured from
And G5 is reported reclaimable, its age measured from that stated time exceeding the declared minimum of 24 hours
And G6 is reported not reclaimable, its age measured from that same stated time being under the declared minimum
And the stated measuring basis is the same one for G5 and G6

Given generation G7 records a completed publication, was created 40 hours ago and was retired 2 hours ago
When the retention policy is evaluated
Then G7 is reported not reclaimable
And the evaluation states that G7's age was measured from its retirement time
And G7's age is not measured from its creation time, which would place it outside the declared minimum

Given generation G8 carries neither a retirement time nor a creation time
When the retention policy is evaluated
Then G8's age is reported as undetermined, naming the times that were absent
And G8 is reported not reclaimable while its age is undetermined
And an undetermined age is not reported as an age that satisfies the declared minimum
```
