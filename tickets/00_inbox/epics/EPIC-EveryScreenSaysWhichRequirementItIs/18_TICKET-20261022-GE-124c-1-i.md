## Implementation Notes

```yaml
- AT MOST ONE FINDING PER (subject, code) IS A STRUCTURAL PROPERTY, NOT A POST-FILTER.
  Key the finding collection by the (subject, code) pair so a duplicate cannot be
  constructed, rather than appending freely and de-duplicating before printing. A
  de-duplication pass at the end is one refactor away from being applied after the
  exit-code decision, or to stdout but not to a machine-readable report, and the mock-data
  artifact states the at-most-one rule as an invariant on the finding set itself.
- PIN COUNT MUST NOT AFFECT FINDING COUNT. One pin, three pins or a dozen pins on
  an undeclared file all yield the same single finding; the pins contribute ac_ids[]
  and message text to that finding, nothing more. The scaling scenario exists because
  a per-surface or per-element loop passes the single-pin case by luck.
- THIS RULE IS PER CODE, NOT PER FILE. A file may legitimately carry one missing-component-declaration
  finding AND one unresolved-ac-id finding (GE-124c-2) — those are different codes
  with different remedies. Do not collapse to one finding per file.
- The deduplication key must be applied identically to the human-readable output and
  to any structured report the check emits, so a reviewer and a downstream consumer
  never see different counts.
```
