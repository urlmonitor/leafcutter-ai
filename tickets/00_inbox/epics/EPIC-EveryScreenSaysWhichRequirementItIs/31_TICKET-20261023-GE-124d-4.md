  section of commit_guardian.json — and state that editing it is a reviewed configuration
  change, not a local convenience. An author who cannot find the list will reach for
  the per-item opt-out instead, which is the wrong tool but the discoverable one.
- 'STATE THE ASYMMETRY EXPLICITLY: the reason lives IN THE FILE (so it appears in
  the diff) and the warn list lives IN CONFIG (because it excuses an area). Readers
  will otherwise assume both are configured in one place, which is the design GE-124d-2
  rejects.'
- THE LAST CLAUSE IS THE POINT AND MUST NOT BE SOFTENED. The failure the PO is guarding
  against is someone adding a directory to the warn list because ONE file in it was
  awkward — silently exempting every other file in that directory, now and in future.
  Say that outcome plainly.
- 'CARRY GE-124g''S BOUNDARY: these escapes govern whether a FILE MUST DECLARE. They
  never change what a declaration is worth, and there is no opt-out from that rule
  (GE-124g-1-i scenario 3). A reader who learns about escapes here is the reader most
  likely to look for one there.'
- change_target stays `code` under the L0's tree-wide instruction; the deliverable
  is the how-to document.
