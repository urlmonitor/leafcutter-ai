```gherkin

  was built for

  that is only ever complete
```

```yaml
  as a c2- or c3-level component diagram, taking its sequence number from scripts/next_diagram_seq.py
  rather than inventing one. It must satisfy check_diagram_naming, check_mermaid_complexity
  and check_mermaid_parent_link, and be linked from docs/architecture/components/ux-prototyping.md.
- 'NAME THE FOUR STORES BY THEIR REAL LOCATIONS: the built application (leafcutter-web/),
  the designs (docs/product-truth/mockups/), the flows (docs/product-truth/flows/),
  and the acceptance-criteria store (docs/acceptance-criteria/). Abstract boxes cannot
  be checked for staleness and cannot be reached from the code.'
- 'MARK THE EXISTING LINKS AS EXISTING AND THE NEW ONES AS NEW, and be accurate about
  which is which. Verified 2026-08-25: mockup.screen -> flow step.screen -> step.implements
  already resolves designs to criteria; the built-page -> design hop (GE-124f-1''s
  data-screen) does NOT exist. Drawing an existing link as new, or the reverse, misleads
  exactly the reader this diagram is for.'
- DRAW THE TWO OPTIONAL LINKS AS OPTIONAL — a page with no design modelled for it
  (GE-124f-1-ii) and a component with no element pins (GE-124b-1 scenario 3). Drawing
  every link as mandatory contradicts both ACs at a glance, and a diagram is the artifact
  people trust over prose when the two disagree.
- DO NOT DRAW A HOP FROM A DECLARATION TO COVERAGE. The chain runs built page -> design
  -> flow step -> criterion; a covering test is a SEPARATE and independent obligation
  (GE-124e-3-i, GE-124g-2). If tests appear on the diagram at all they must be visibly
  off the chain, or the picture itself teaches the substitution GE-124g exists to
  block.
- c3-005-artifact-knowledge-graph.md already draws part of this artifact graph — extend
  or cross-reference it rather than drawing a second, competing picture of the same
  stores.
- change_target stays `code` under the L0's tree-wide instruction; the deliverable
  is the diagram document.
```
