## Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Given drive A and drive B are running concurrently in their own independent clones,
When drive A's process is killed part-way through writing a commit (leaving a
  half-written or zero-length loose object in drive A's own object store),
Then drive B's object store, refs, and index remain intact and usable, and drive B
  can continue committing and complete its work with no error.
And any damage is confined to drive A's own object store (which is discarded when
  that drive is removed), so no shared store shared with drive B is affected.
```
