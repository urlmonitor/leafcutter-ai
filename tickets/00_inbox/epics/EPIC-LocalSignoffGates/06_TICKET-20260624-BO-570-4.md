## Acceptance Criteria

Given a ticket whose changed surface includes one or more Python files,
  and python-coder has finished its implementation work,
When python-coder evaluates its sign-off for the ticket,

When python-coder evaluates its sign-off,
Then the ruff check passes,
And the sign-off may proceed to "ok" on this criterion,
And the lint failures are caught before the change reaches commit, the PR,
  or CI, rather than surfacing for the first time in CI.
