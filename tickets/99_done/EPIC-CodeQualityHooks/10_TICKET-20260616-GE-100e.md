## Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin

  enabled: true
  strict: false
  min_coverage_percent: 80
  coverage_xml_path: "coverage.xml"
  compare_branch: "origin/main"
  max_age_seconds: 3600

  origin/main measures at 65% (below the 80% threshold)
When the diff-cover hook runs at pre-commit time
Then the hook emits a warning listing the files with insufficient coverage
  and their individual coverage percentages
And the warning states the overall diff coverage (65%) vs the threshold (80%)
And because strict is false, the hook exits with code 0 (warn-only)
```
