# The steps a close is checked against are the ones the ticket's own record demands, and the caller cannot change that list

## Actor / Goal

As the leafcutter-ai system, I want to implement AC `BO-400e-1` — The steps a close is checked against are the ones the ticket's own record demands, and the caller cannot change that list — so that the acceptance criterion is satisfied.

## Context

## Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Given a ticket whose own record names nine phases as needed,
  And exactly one of those nine -- call it the ninth -- carries no sign-off anywhere in
    that record,
  And three separate attempts to close that same ticket, identical in every respect
    except what the party asking for the close presents alongside the request:
      the first presents a list of the other eight and describes the ninth as excluded
        from this run,
      the second presents a list of ten -- the same eight plus two phases the ticket's
        record never names at all,
      the third presents no list whatsoever,
When each attempt reaches the point at which the finished state would be written,
Then all three decisions are taken against the nine phases the ticket's record names,
  And in all three the ninth phase counts as demanded and is found unaccounted for,
  And all three attempts are refused, and each refusal names the ninth phase,
  And the two phases the record never names are treated as demanded in none of the
    three,
  And after all three the ticket's recorded state is unchanged,
  And the three attempts produce the same outcome as each other, because the thing that
    differed between them was never an input to the decision,
So the list of demanded steps is read out of the record every time, and the party whose
  behaviour is being checked can neither narrow it, widen it, nor supply it.
```
