## Implementation Notes

```yaml
- The step reuses the existing stage-end rule-check runner of /plan-feature (the one
  that runs after po, ba, itpo and test-design) with --stage itpo-handback and --before-ref
  HEAD, where HEAD is the batch as committed before the hand-back pass; no second
  runner and no agent-side judgement of the result.
- 'Fail-closed as for the other stages (ACD-2500a-1): the stage passes only on exit
  0, verdict passed and records_checked above 0; a dispatch error or unreadable report
  is a failed stage.'
- On failure the findings (file, family, message) are shown to the user and the run
  stops before the final gate; the hand-back stage is reported not complete and nothing
  is committed as approved.
- 'templates/workflows-js/plan-feature.js is over its size limit and its measured
  length must not grow (GE-127b-1 growth ratchet): lines added are paid for by lines
  removed or by rationale comments moved to docs/reference/plan-feature-workflow-rationale*.md.
  Build after GE-127h-1 so decision-history lines are free.'
- Canonical sources only; deployed and installed copies are regenerated by the build
  and never hand-edited (ADR-001).
- Before the hand-back round, /plan-feature commits the test-design output (test_spec,
  test-design authorship, file_findings and their opened-files lists) as its own stage
  commit, so that --before-ref HEAD for --stage itpo-handback is the post-design batch.
  Without it every designer field would read as a hand-back edit. If a commit is not
  possible, the step passes an equivalent post-design --before-snapshot instead; it
  never runs the hand-back check against the pre-design batch.
```
