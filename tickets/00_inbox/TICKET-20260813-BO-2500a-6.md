# A done composite AC derives its proof from its children, not from a direct linked test

## Actor / Goal

As the leafcutter-ai system, I want to implement AC `BO-2500a-6` — A done composite AC derives its proof from its children, not from a direct linked test — so that the acceptance criterion is satisfied.

## Context

This ticket was generated from AC store entry `BO-2500a-6`. Component: `build-orchestration`. Assigned agent: `python-coder`. Estimated complexity: `M`. Complexity: `medium`.

## Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Given a work_status: done composite AC (an AC that has children or a non-empty
  covered_by) whose child ACs are each covered by a passing "# covers:" test,
When the proof-of-done coverage check is run in its CI-changed mode against
  that composite,
Then the check treats the composite as satisfied because its proof is derived
  from its covered children,
And the check does NOT fail the composite for lacking a direct "# covers:" test
  of its own,
And a work_status: done leaf AC (one with no children and an empty covered_by)
  still fails the check unless it has its own passing "# covers:" test.
```

- [ ] AC-1: the check treats the composite as satisfied because its proof is derived
- [ ] AC-2: the check does NOT fail the composite for lacking a direct "# covers:" test
- [ ] AC-3: a work_status: done leaf AC (one with no children and an empty covered_by)
