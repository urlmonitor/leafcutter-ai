## Implementation Notes

```yaml
- 'One click on another node while a drawer is open reaches that node: the full-canvas
  scrim in leafcutter-web/components/flows/flow-drawer.tsx / flow-explorer.tsx lets
  node clicks through while a click on empty canvas still closes the drawer (UXP-591-4-ii).
  The mechanism (pointer-events pass-through, hit-testing the click, or no scrim over
  the canvas) is the coder''s choice.'
- 'The switch is one replace to flowHref(flowId, newStepId): a single ''step'' query
  parameter (set, never appended) and no intermediate close-then-open history write.'
- jsdom does no hit-testing, so a component test that clicks the node element directly
  cannot prove the scrim lets the click through; the pass-through is proven by this
  AC's Playwright spec with a real click in Chromium against `next start` (owner decision
  2026-10-09).
```
