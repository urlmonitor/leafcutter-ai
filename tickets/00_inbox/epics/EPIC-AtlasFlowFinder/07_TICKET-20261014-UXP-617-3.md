# Every step and branch card on a flow page shows who runs it: Code, AI agent, Person or Jev

## Actor / Goal

As the leafcutter-ai system, I want to implement AC `UXP-617-3` — Every step and branch card on a flow page shows who runs it: Code, AI agent, Person or Jev — so that the acceptance criterion is satisfied.

## Context

This ticket was generated from AC store entry `UXP-617-3`. Component: `ux-prototyping`. Assigned agent: `frontend-coder`. Estimated complexity: `M`. Complexity: `medium`.

## Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Given leafcutter/decision-retrieval has steps run by code, by Jev and by a person,
When a person opens /flows/leafcutter/decision-retrieval,
Then every step card and every branch card carries an actor badge,
And 'Ask Jev whether each applies' shows 'Jev', 'Reuse it, or decide anew?' shows 'Person'
  and 'Look up matching records' shows 'Code',
And the 'Reuse it, or decide anew?' card's accessible name is
  'Step 4 of 5: Reuse it, or decide anew? (Person)', and a branch card's accessible name has
  the form 'Branch: <label> (<actor>)',
And a step that Jev runs is never badged 'AI agent',
And the badge words are the same four the finder's Actor filter and step rows use: Code,
  AI agent, Person and Jev.
```

- [ ] AC-2: 'Ask Jev whether each applies' shows 'Jev', 'Reuse it, or decide anew?' shows 'Person'
- [ ] AC-3: 'Look up matching records' shows 'Code'
- [ ] AC-4: the 'Reuse it, or decide anew?' card's accessible name is
- [ ] AC-5: a step that Jev runs is never badged 'AI agent'
- [ ] AC-6: the badge words are the same four the finder's Actor filter and step rows use: Code

  cards, the finder's step rows and the Actor facet. Jev is never folded into llm.

  <label> (<actor>)'' for a step, ''Branch: <label> (<actor>)'' for a branch.'

  ratchet); move the badge into components/flows/parts/ (2 files today) rather than

  what it shows is an open owner decision.
