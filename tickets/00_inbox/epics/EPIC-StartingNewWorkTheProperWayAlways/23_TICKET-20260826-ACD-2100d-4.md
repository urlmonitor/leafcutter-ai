- THIS IS A REFERENCE PAGE -- THE LAYOUT AS IT IS -- NOT A TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE.
  The reader arrives having already been surprised, but what they need is the fact
  that resolves their confusion, not a procedure. Route the 'what do I do now' half
  to the known-issues entries and to the behaviours that fix them; a restated procedure
  is a second account that drifts from the governed one.

  started from a worktree, started from a directory outside the project -- not around
  the two directory trees. A page organised around the layouts is accurate and useless
  to someone who does not yet know which layout they are in, which is the whole population
  this page serves.

  THE RECORD THAT DELIVERS IT. Do not write any of it from intent ahead of the behaviour
  landing. A reference page that documents what the route is going to do becomes wrong
  on the day the implementation differs and nothing in the repository detects it --
  unlike the behaviours themselves, which have tests.

  deliverable), never from a hand-written list of directories. A second list is exactly

  a message on their screen and matching it to this page; a paraphrase they cannot
