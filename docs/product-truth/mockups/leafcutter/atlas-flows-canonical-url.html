Canonical, shareable flow URLs

Every flow lives at /flows/<flow id>, and every step or branch at /flows/<flow id>?step=<step id>. Opening, selecting or drilling updates the address bar; back and forward follow the selection; an unknown flow id shows a 404 notice and an unknown step id a "Step not found" notice.

Drawer actions replace the address; drill and select push

Sessions nav-drawer-replace, nav-drawer-in-history and nav-select-from-open-drawer (FlowNavigation). Opening a step's drawer writes ?step=<id> into the address bar and closing it removes it, both with history 'replace': the Entries count does not grow (+0), so Back is never spent on drawers, and the current URL always shares exactly what you see. Drilling and selecting another flow still push a new entry (+1). Owner decision, mockup gate 2026-10-09.
