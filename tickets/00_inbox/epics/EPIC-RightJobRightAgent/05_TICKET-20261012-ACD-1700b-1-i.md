## Implementation Notes

```yaml
- Split detection derives from the set of DISTINCT crafts the map (config/surface_craft_map.yaml)
  resolves across the AC's declared surfaces (data-driven), not from a hard-coded
  surface-pair list; two surfaces resolving to two different crafts trigger a split
  flag.
- A single assigned_agent must NOT be accepted as covering two distinct-craft surfaces
  (e.g. a .py helper AND templates/agents/*.md prose).
- The split flag must enumerate each surface and its resolved craft so the recommended
  split is actionable.
```
