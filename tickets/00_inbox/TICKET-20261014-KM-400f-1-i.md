## Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Given the declared retention bound is at most 3 retained generations and a minimum retirement age of 24 hours
And the repository holds 12 generation records, every one of which records a failed publication and none of which records a completed publication
And 123,810 entity nodes are tagged to those 12 generations
When the retention policy is evaluated
Then all 12 generation records are reported, each with its own reclaimability verdict and the reason for that verdict
And the evaluation reports that it assessed 12 generation records
And no generation record is left out of the report on the grounds that its publication did not complete

Given the repository holds 1 generation record whose publication completed and 11 whose publication failed
When the retention policy is evaluated
Then the evaluation reports that it assessed 12 generation records
And one further generation record whose publication failed is added, and on the next evaluation the reported figure is 13
And the reported figure moves with the number of generation records present, for every publication outcome a record can carry

Given the repository holds no generation record at all
When the retention policy is evaluated
Then the evaluation reports that it assessed 0 generation records and found 0 reclaimable
And that result is distinguishable in the report from an evaluation that assessed 12 generation records and found 0 reclaimable
```
