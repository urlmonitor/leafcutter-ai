```gherkin
Then the Atlas answers with the same permanent redirect (308) a known flow id gets, whose
  location is the canonical form with the '/' in the flow id literal:

  'leafcutter/flow-render-pipline' verbatim, with no flow drawn, an empty breadcrumb and the
  flow selector at Source Real, Kind User and Realization All,
And for the first link no drawer opens and no 'Step not found' notice shows,
And neither link lands on /flows or draws leafcutter/deliver-a-feature or any other flow,
And the address bar and the tab's history hold the canonical form, not the old /flows?flow=
  address.
```
