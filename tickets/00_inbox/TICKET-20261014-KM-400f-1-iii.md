## Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Given the declared retention bound is at most 3 retained generations and a minimum retirement age of 24 hours
And the active pointer references generation G12
And G12 records a publication that did not complete, because publication was interrupted part way through
And G12 was created 40 hours ago and carries no retirement time
When the retention policy is evaluated
Then G12 is reported not reclaimable because it is the generation the active pointer references
And that verdict holds whether or not G12's publication completed
And the evaluation reports that the active pointer references a generation whose publication did not complete

Given the active pointer references generation G12 whose publication completed
And generation G11 records a failed publication, was created 40 hours ago and sits outside the declared count bound
When the retention policy is evaluated
Then G12 is reported not reclaimable because it is the generation the active pointer references
And G11 is reported reclaimable
And the reason given for G12 names the active pointer, not its publication outcome

Given the active pointer references no generation
When the retention policy is evaluated
Then the evaluation reports that no active generation could be identified
And it does not treat the most recently created generation as active in place of the absent pointer
And no generation is reported reclaimable while the active generation is unidentified
```
