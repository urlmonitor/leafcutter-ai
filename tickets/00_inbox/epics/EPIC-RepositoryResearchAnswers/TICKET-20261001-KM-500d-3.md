## Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Given a versioned representative set of real project research cases has independently reviewed expected outcomes and source references
When the current retrieval behavior and a proposed query or ranking improvement are evaluated on the same declared cases and source scope
Then the report distinguishes exact lookup, relationships, ambiguous research, no-match, correction and unsupported-data cases according to the available corpus
And it records case counts, source and query versions, applicable model settings, expected and actual results, provenance validity, retrieval quality, content size and latency
And custom registered queries are evaluated through the same validated catalog entry point used by research
And partial, unavailable, denied or not-run cases remain visible with explicit denominators rather than being counted as correct empty answers
And mandatory quality and latency gates are chosen from a measured baseline before deciding whether the proposed change may be promoted
And the comparison preserves reviewed assessment cases rather than treating candidate-authored passing examples as independent quality evidence
And an improvement is described as a governed query, ranking or knowledge change only to the extent actually evaluated
And query reuse does not claim model-weight training, autonomous rewriting of canonical knowledge or proven semantic usefulness from synthetic vectors alone
And insufficient real corpus, model access or authorization leaves the relevant measurement not-run or limited rather than inventing a score.
And the first source-anchored batch identifies its twelve planned positive/control cases across six of the eight role-query families, without calling that sample a representative benchmark of all twenty-five questions
And required-field meaning, exact clauses, scope inclusion, complete versus truncated counts, declared versus executed proof, missing query versus missing source support, and execution versus question fulfillment are assessed against their independent expected outcomes
And missing or withheld work status cannot be replaced by lifecycle status, a shortened criterion cannot be accepted as complete, and a truncated dependent list cannot become an exact total
And test references without execution evidence cannot pass an executed-proof check, unsupported mappings cannot pass a query-build eligibility check, and absent trace evidence cannot pass a remote-observation check
And expected source-oracle values are kept separate from the actual tool response and cannot fill missing response fields during assessment
And planned, runnable, executed, passed, failed and not-run cases remain distinguishable; a specification alone has no passing result
And expected query-family coverage and uncovered role questions remain visible when readiness or implementation-trace cases are deferred beyond the initial batch
And a changed answer meaning, required field, query behavior or source mapping invalidates any affected prior comparison until its expectations are reviewed again.
```
