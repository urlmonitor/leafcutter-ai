## Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Given a run driven far enough to reach each of the five points at which the route
  asks for a decision -- approving a drafted product-truth artifact; approving
  the mid-pipeline authoring stage; giving final approval; choosing how to
  proceed when the request looks already covered; and deciding what to do with
  stranded drafts from an earlier run,
When each of those points is reached,
Then the question is put on the channel that reaches the person running the
  route,
And the run's own record of every dispatch it made contains no dispatch whose
  purpose was to obtain an answer to any of the five,
And the run does not move past any of the five until an answer has arrived on
  that channel.
```

## Implementation Notes

```yaml
- The change must land under templates/. The deployed workflow is build output.
- The class must be closed by one mechanism covering every decision whose answer is
  a human choice -- not by five per-site edits. Two of the five sites appear in no
  known issue, and one of those can delete drafts; per-site patching is how those
  two get missed.
- A new decision point added later must inherit the rule by virtue of being a human-decision
  gate, not by someone remembering to route it. A mechanism that must be opted into
  at each site is the same defect deferred.
- 'The property must be observable from outside the run: the run''s own record of
  what it dispatched is the evidence. A test that asserts a particular agent name
  is absent from the source passes as soon as the dispatch target is built from a
  variable.'
- The route must not proceed past a decision point on a timeout or a default. What
  happens when the channel reaches nobody is ACD-2100c-2's subject and must be reached,
  not bypassed.
- Must satisfy the repository error-handling policy on the channel interaction, which
  is external I/O.
- Must not change what any of the five decisions offers or means -- only who is asked.
  The covered-route and orphan-resolution decisions keep their existing choices.
```
