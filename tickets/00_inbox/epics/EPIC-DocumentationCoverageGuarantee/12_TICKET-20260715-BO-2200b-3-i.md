## Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Given the documentation-verifier's placeholder detection,
When a required doc's changed content consists only of a heading with no body, or
  contains only residual template tokens such as `{summary}` or `<placeholder>`,
Then the verifier treats it as placeholder content and reports `status: blocker`.
And when a required doc's changed content is short but genuine -- real prose or a real
  diagram body with no placeholder markers -- the verifier does NOT reject it for being
  brief, and returns `status: ok`.
```
