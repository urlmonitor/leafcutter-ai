## Context

This ticket was generated from AC store entry `BO-1600d-3-v`. Component: `build-orchestration`. Assigned agent: `python-coder`. Estimated complexity: `M`.

## Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Given a linked worktree's index cache-tree is poisoned such that an in-place
  cache-tree rebuild in that worktree does not clear the corruption
When the recovery step engine addresses the poisoned worktree
Then its printed plan includes creating a fresh worktree (from the now-repaired
  object store / branch) as the fallback, and on confirmation it rebuilds via that
  fresh worktree rather than continuing to fight the poisoned one
And after the fresh worktree is established, a status/read in it succeeds with no
  cache-tree error
And the fallback is presented as a distinct, heavier action in the dry-run plan (so
  the human sees a new worktree will be created) — it is not applied silently
```
