## Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Given the `### documentation-expert` subsection is being generated for a documented AC,
When the generator writes that AC's `- [ ] AC-N:` line,
Then the line names the Diataxis genre required -- one of `how-to`, `reference-doc`,

And the line names the target documentation path where the doc belongs.

  writer knows specifically what the doc must cover rather than receiving a generic nudge.
```
