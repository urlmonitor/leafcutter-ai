## Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Given three spot-check reviewers are dispatched against one resolved review scope
When each reviewer is given its instructions
Then the three reviewers are each seeded with a different review angle, and the three
  angles together are:
    - edge cases and boundary inputs (empty, null, oversized, malformed, extreme values)
    - integration points and error handling (the seams where the feature meets the rest
      of the system, and how it behaves when those seams fail)
    - user-facing behavior and usability (how the feature behaves in real use from the
      perspective of the person invoking it)
And no two reviewers are seeded with the same angle
And each reviewer probes the feature only from its assigned angle
```
