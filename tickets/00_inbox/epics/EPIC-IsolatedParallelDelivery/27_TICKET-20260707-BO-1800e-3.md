## Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Given a developer wants to update their local `main` to match the hub,
When the sync runs,
Then it performs a `fetch` followed by a fast-forward-only update of local `main`
  (no local commits, no merge commits, no rebasing local work onto `main`),
And if local `main` has diverged such that a fast-forward is not possible, the sync
  stops with a clear message rather than creating a merge or force-moving `main`.
```
