## Implementation Notes

```yaml
- The section is written into `docs/reference/artifact-numbering.md`, the file GE-122a-3
  creates and GE-122b-4 extends. Third section, same document — a separate bypass
  document is how a contributor hitting a block ends up in the wrong file.
- 'The authoring-time stage''s bypass status must be stated accurately and it is not
  obvious: that stage is neither invoked nor skipped by the contributor, and it can
  fail SILENTLY — a PostToolUse hook that crashes, times out or cannot import its
  dependency emits nothing, which is indistinguishable from a clean write. ''Cannot
  be bypassed on purpose but can be absent without anyone noticing'' is the true statement,
  and a reader who believes it always runs is worse informed than one who knows it
  might not.'
- Do not assert that the shared build is unbypassable until GE-122d-2's required-check
  step has actually landed. Until then it is a failing report, not a block, and documenting
  the intent as the state is the same class of error as ADR-029's claim that an unregistered
  hook was wired.
- The cannot-establish section must describe the AMENDED disposition, not ADR-029's
  superseded fail-open one. Cross-reference the amending decision record so a reader
  who finds ADR-029 first is not misled by it.
- The non-bypass clause (a commit proceeding after a guard defect that occurred post-read)
  must be stated as a designed disposition with its reason, and explicitly not as
  an escape hatch a contributor can invoke. An undocumented case of that shape reads
  to a later contributor as either a bug or a hidden loophole, and both readings cause
  harm.
- Doc frontmatter, description and length are mechanically enforced; if the three-section
  document exceeds the doc-length gate, split by section into linked documents rather
  than trimming the dispositions — the per-stage differences are the content.
```
