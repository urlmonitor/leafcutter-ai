## Implementation Notes

```yaml
- 'IT PO decision (the BA''s open question): records written and read back cleanly
  in a failed run keep readiness approved on disk. Because the step is idempotent,
  a re-run converges; they are not rolled back, and the failure report names them
  as written but not committed.'
- A record whose read-back fails is restored to its exact pre-write bytes before the
  step exits.
- 'The workflow treats a non-zero exit or an unreadable report from the approval step
  as a failed run: it makes no stage commit, and the closing summary names the failure.'
- The failure is logged at error severity, naming the file and the reason.
```
