## Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Given a person asks which candidate requirement should be built next or whether a promised benefit is ready
And the request identifies a decision policy and supplies some combination of recorded priority, readiness, work status, dependencies and proof
When research provides its recommendation
Then it cites the available source facts and identifies the policy used to compare the candidates or assess the benefit
And recommendation or interpretation is labeled separately from canonical priority and recorded status
And missing prerequisites, unresolved dependency evidence and absent execution or deployment proof remain visible for each affected conclusion
And a requirement marked done is not described as available to users without evidence for the requested environment
And if the ranking policy or required source facts are unavailable, the response requests the missing decision input or reports that the ranking cannot be established
And no recommendation silently changes canonical priorities, readiness or work status.
```
