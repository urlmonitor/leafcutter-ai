## Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Given any example artifact or any example acceptance criterion,
When it is read on its own, by a person or by an agent that knows nothing about where it came
  from,
Then it states the product it belongs to and that that product is an example,
And that statement is held in the artifact itself rather than inferred from its location,
And an artifact of the project's own record read the same way carries no such statement,
And an artifact whose stated product disagrees with the product root it lives under is
  reported, naming both.
```
