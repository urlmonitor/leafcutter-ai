description: "Four known issues were selected as owned by the build-feature surface and put through a product-owner analysis each, with the first question being whether the store already covered them rather than what to write. That question paid for itself immediately. KI-SS-001, the only blocker of the four, needed no authoring at all: BO-3100 was written the previous day explicitly against it, its goal-level record names the register entry as the blocker it closes, its notes carry the durable invariant verbatim, and twenty-four of its twenty-four leaf criteria are already approved with an assigned agent, technical requirements and a test contract. It needs building, not specifying. That is recorded here because the register's own status field does not mention BO-3100, so the next reader of the register would have re-authored the whole tree — which is the duplicate-work risk this exercise was set up to test for, and it very nearly caught the operator too. Three of the four pre-allocated identifier blocks went unused for the same reason: every remaining gap belonged in a family that already existed. For KI-BO-025, where the epic driver asks its planner for one set of ready work and never asks again — seventeen of thirty-seven tickets scheduled, and every ticket carrying any prerequisite silently dropped — two feature criteria were added rather than one, and the product owner argued they are not alternatives. One carries the work through every layer of its prerequisites in a single run; the other requires that work a run left unbuilt is counted and named. The second holds whether or not the first is ever built, because runs stop early for reasons unrelated to layering, and telling an operator to type the same command five times is honest but poor. For KI-BO-007, where a phase is counted complete though the agent halted without doing it, two criteria target the half that the earlier tool-allowlist fix did not close: a step counts as done only when the thing it exists to produce actually exists, and a step that stopped short is reported as stopped rather than finished. The generic emptiness check already specified elsewhere cannot reach this, and the reason is precise: its evidence surface is any change to tracked or untracked files, and the anchor incident PASSES that test while still failing, because the pull-request agent drafted its pull request, wrote into the ticket record, halted before pushing and returned cleanly. Files changed; nothing it existed to produce came into being. For KI-BO-028, where acceptance criteria marked done are falsified by defects recorded in the same register, a new goal covers the standing gap rather than the individual instances: nothing in the store reads the known-issues register as evidence against the acceptance-criteria store in either direction, so the two can contradict each other indefinitely. The nearest existing coverage measures done-ness by whether a passing tagged test exists, which is blind to six of the eight instances found. Fifty-one testable children were then decomposed beneath the ten new feature criteria, every parent at or under the enforced cap of five. Two existing records changed. BP-1100f was reopened from done: its criteria promise, in benefit language, exactly the fix KI-BO-007 is still open for — confirming that a change actually happened and its real result can be observed, rather than treating a step having run as proof — while only one of its six children sits on the effect side, and that one keys on a field set on thirty-five of roughly two thousand nine hundred records. Its implemented-by list was empty, so the done claim could not be audited from the record at all. The six children are not reopened, since each did its own job, and the note forbids the tempting reconciliation of narrowing the parent's criteria to match what the children already deliver, which would relax the requirement to fit the implementation. BO-300a-5's criteria were amended to remove a specification defect that shipped: they required unbuilt work to be reported as discovered after planning, a cause no run can establish because nothing records what the epic contained when the plan was fixed, so a faithful implementation necessarily mislabels work the planner simply never selected. The criteria now permit a cause only where the run can establish one and require an explicit undetermined otherwise. The load-bearing half is untouched and slightly strengthened, with the set-comparison rule promoted from technical guidance into the criteria body where it binds. A third record, one of the newly authored ones, carried the same internal contradiction and was corrected before decomposition; two analysts reached that finding independently. Three commit-time guards each caught something real on the way in — a missing author attribution, eight missing parent back-links at the third tier, and a side-effect declaration — and the third of those was a false positive worth recording: the deriver that decides whether criteria assert a durable effect slices from the first Then clause to the end of the text, so in a multi-scenario record every later Given clause is scanned too, contradicting its own documented promise that a phrase in a Given does not count. Two incidental phrases were reworded to clear it, with a note in the record stating plainly that this is the honest fix for that record and not a fix for the deriver."
