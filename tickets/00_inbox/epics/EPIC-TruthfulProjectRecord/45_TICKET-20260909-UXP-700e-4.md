## Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Given a person about to add a bound to the record's shape, or a person who has just been told
  their artifact exceeds one,
When they open the reference on the record's size bounds,
Then it lists every declared bound, its value, the field it applies to and the shape version
  in which it took effect,
And it states the rollout order a new bound must follow - declare behind a new shape version,

  edit when the two disagree,

  description cap would fail all 14 journeys as they stand.
```
