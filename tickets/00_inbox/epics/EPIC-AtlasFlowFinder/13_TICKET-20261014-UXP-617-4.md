# A how-to guide teaches a person to find a flow or step with the finder and share the search

## Context

This ticket was generated from AC store entry `UXP-617-4`. Component: `ux-prototyping`. Assigned agent: `documentation-expert`. Estimated complexity: `S`. Complexity: `low`.

## Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Given the finder at /flows searches, filters, shares its search by address and opens results,
When a person who has never used it reads the how-to guide for the Atlas Flows finder,
Then the guide walks through one worked example from start to finish: typing
  'selected option' (5 flows, 27 steps match), ticking 'Person' under Actor
  (2 flows, 5 steps match), copying /flows?q=selected+option&actor=human to share it, and
  opening the step row 'Reuse it, or decide anew?',
And it states the search rule: case is ignored, and a step matches when every typed word
  starts a word of one of its label, text, AC ids, input or output field names, screen or
  entities,
And it states that values combine with OR within one filter and with AND across filters, and
  that each count is the number of flows that value alone would list,
And it shows how to find steps whose inputs or outputs are not defined yet with
  'I/O not yet defined' (/flows?io=gap),
And it explains the 'No flows match' state and its 'Remove <filter>' suggestions,
And it explains that Back from an opened result returns the finder with the same search,
  and that a filter change on a flow page never swaps the drawn flow but shows
  'Outside the current filters'.
```
