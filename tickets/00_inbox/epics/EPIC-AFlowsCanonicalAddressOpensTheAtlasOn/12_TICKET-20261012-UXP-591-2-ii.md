## Implementation Notes

```yaml
- 'Opening a real drawer from the ''Step not found'' state goes through the same replace-entry
  path as any drawer open (UXP-591-4): one history replace to flowHref(flowId, newStepId),
  never a push and never a second write.'
- Because the notice derives from the address, that replace is what clears it; there
  is no separate clear call that could leave the notice and the address out of step.
- The replace rewrites the entry's stored view state together with its URL, so Back
  after a later push restores ?step=docenforce, never ?step=lint.
```
