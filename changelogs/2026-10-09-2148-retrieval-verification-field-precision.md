## Entry

Questions about what verification must demonstrate require acceptance clauses
and the authored test specification as distinct facts. Explicit requests for
acceptance clauses, a specification, references or status alone remain narrow.
Declared reference values do not themselves request linked Test records, and
possible discovery leads do not become required answer fields.

The existing approved AC remains in progress. The new 16/16 result measures
interpretation only; live retrieval correctness is a separate pending gate.
