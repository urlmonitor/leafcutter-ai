description: "No behaviour changes to any guardrail — this lands the build plan for GE-120 (green means checked) plus one schema field and a known-issues batch. The epic was produced by scripts/goal_to_epic.py --ac GE-120, which walks the approved AC tree and emits one ticket per leaf: 37 tickets, topologically ordered, with inter-ticket depends_on translated from AC ids to co-located ticket filenames, and a Master_Plan carrying the dependency map. Before generating, three depends_on edges were added by hand to GE-120b-1, GE-120b-4 and GE-120b-1-i, each of which already declared the same edge in expects_from — a field goal_to_epic.py never reads. Two of those name GE-120c-1, the out-of-process harness that b-1 and b-4 are verified through, so without them the sequencer was free to schedule the dependents ahead of the harness; with them, c-1 lands at position 12 ahead of b-1 at 13 and b-4 at 15. criteria were untouched on all three and each carries an amended_by entry. The schema change is the load-bearing part: goal_to_epic.py writes a target_epic field into every AC it processes and reads it back on a re-run to decide whether that AC already belongs to an epic, but config/ac_store_schema.json sets additionalProperties: false and had never heard of it, so all 37 records failed validate_ac_schema.py — and AC store valid is a required check on main. A store-wide grep found target_epic on exactly those 37 records and nowhere else, meaning goal_to_epic.py --ac has never produced a committed epic in this repository and the incompatibility had simply never had the chance to surface. The field is deliberate and functional, so the schema was extended rather than the data stripped. Five known issues are filed against ac-driven-dev. KI-ACD-013 records the schema collision above and notes the residual: nothing runs validate_ac_schema over goal_to_epic's output, so the same drift can recur with the next field either side adds. KI-ACD-014 records that the generator wrote absolute filesystem paths into implemented_by on all 37 records; the link targets were correct and were correctly re-pointed after the tickets moved into the epic folder, so the defect is a missing relative_to at the write rather than a path-tracking error, and the data has been corrected to repo-relative with every back-link verified to resolve. KI-ACD-015 records the expects_from blindness and deliberately leaves open which of the two fields is authoritative, because implementing either half without that decision produces two sources of truth for build order. KI-ACD-016 records that generated tickets repeat their criteria as a checklist built by splitting wrapped block scalars on And, so every item ends mid-clause; the full criteria remain intact in the gherkin block above and are what ac-validator reads. KI-ACD-017 records that the run took about thirty minutes for 37 tickets with no incremental output, during which an intermediate state was misread as a duplicate-ticket defect — a long silent run invites wrong conclusions about its own correctness. KI-ACD-018 records the largest of the reference defects, found when ticket_frontmatter_guard rejected the commit: depends_on is translated before the tickets are moved into the epic folder and never re-pointed afterwards, so all 27 inter-ticket edges across 20 tickets named the pre-move filename and resolved to nothing. The ordinal prefixes still encode the right order, so the Master_Plan reads correctly to a human while the machine-readable edge a dependency-aware drive would use is dangling — all 37 would have looked unblocked. All 27 were corrected, in the ticket frontmatter and in the Master_Plan table, and each verified to resolve. Contrast with KI-ACD-014: the generator did correctly re-point implemented_by to the post-move path in the same run, so it knows the final filenames and simply applies that to one surface and not the other two. KI-ACD-012 was also confirmed a second time — the generated Master_Plan was rejected for the six frontmatter fields that entry already documents; those were added by hand here, along with type: epic, and status corrected from in_progress to todo. None of the 37 tickets has been built; every GE-120 record remains work_status: todo."
