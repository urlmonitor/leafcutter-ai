## Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Given a doc-triggering ticket whose agents map was generated with `documentation-expert`
  and `documentation-verifier` both `needed`,
When someone hand-edits the ticket to set `documentation-verifier` (or
  `documentation-expert`) to `not_needed` and the ticket is re-generated or re-validated,
Then the status is restored to `needed` for both agents.
And the restore is silent-proof: the change is either overwritten or surfaced as a
  validation error, never silently accepted.
```
