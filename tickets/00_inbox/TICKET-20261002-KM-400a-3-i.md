  same atomic publication or rollback operation; preserve pinned reads of retired
  generations and fail closed on interrupted migration.

  read-only enforcement, repository scope and generation scope.

  preserve canonical keys, raw payloads, source revisions, entity and relationship
  counts, active pointers, historical generations and retrieval results; reject malformed
  or foreign records and cross-generation edges rather than partially rewriting them.

  kernel evidence contracts; do not require credentials for offline validation or
  publish new content during a representation-only migration.
