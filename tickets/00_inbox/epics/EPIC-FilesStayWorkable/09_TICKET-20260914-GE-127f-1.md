```gherkin

  its permitted length,

  leaving it standing at exactly the 2,677 it stood at before,
When the author performs an ordinary commit,

  file and gives back only what it took is no longer sufficient,

  leaves it standing at exactly 400,
When the author performs an ordinary commit,
Then the commit completes — the required length is the less demanding of the
  permitted length and the previous length minus the lines added, so 60 lines
  leaving the file discharges the change and 100 is never asked for,

  leaving it standing at 401,
When the author performs an ordinary commit,

  it and removes three, leaving it standing at 2,674,

  permitted length of 400, and a change that adds 12 measured lines to it and

When the author performs an ordinary commit,
Then the commit completes and no line of the outcome names that file — a file
  at or below its permitted length is not in this population at all.
```
