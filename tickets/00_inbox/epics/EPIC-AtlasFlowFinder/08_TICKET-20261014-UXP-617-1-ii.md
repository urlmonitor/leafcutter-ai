# Results are grouped by flow, each card showing up to three matching step rows with an actor badge and why it matched

## Actor / Goal

As the leafcutter-ai system, I want to implement AC `UXP-617-1-ii` — Results are grouped by flow, each card showing up to three matching step rows with an actor badge and why it matched — so that the acceptance criterion is satisfied.

## Context

This ticket was generated from AC store entry `UXP-617-1-ii`. Component: `ux-prototyping`. Assigned agent: `frontend-coder`. Estimated complexity: `M`. Complexity: `medium`.

## Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Given /flows?q=selected+option&actor=human is open,
When the results show,
Then Publishing the decision record comes first with 4 matching steps and three step rows,
  'Decide to publish', 'Commit and review in git' and 'Merge to main', in step order,
And each of those rows carries a 'Person' badge and the reason it matched, either
  'Input · Decision record › selected_option_id' or
  'Output · Decision record › selected_option_id',
And the card ends with '1 more step',
And Retrieving a decision as precedent follows with its one row 'Reuse it, or decide anew?',
And no flow card shows more than three step rows, listing steps in step order before branches,
And with a search, flows whose own name matches come first, then flows with more matching
  steps, then flows by name.
```

- [ ] AC-3: the card ends with '1 more step'

## Implementation Notes

```yaml
- 'Result ordering (with q: flow-own match first, then more matching steps, then name;
  without q: product then name, case ignored), the three-row cap (steps in step order,
  then branches in stored order), more_steps and the reason ranking are computed in
  leafcutter-web/lib/flow-finder/, not in the component.'
- 'The reason ranking follows the dataset invariant exactly: Step/Branch label, AC,
  Output, Input, Screen, Entity, then Text; without q the chosen step facet first
  of produces, uses, io, screen and actor.'
- Actor badge text comes from the shared actor-label mapping of UXP-617-3; no local
  label table.
- Each step row and flow card is a real link whose href is built by flowHref from
  lib/flow-url.ts (/flows/<flow id>[?step=<node id>]), so open-in-new-tab works; the
  same-tab behaviour is UXP-617-2-i.
- Expected values come from the leafcutter/atlas-flows v4 dataset records, replayed
  against a frozen finder-index fixture in leafcutter-web/fixtures/flow-finder/ produced
  by the finder's own index builder from docs/product-truth/flows/ at the dataset's
  measurement commit (provenance recorded in the fixture); tests never pin counts
  against the live store, which grows. fixtures/** is already excluded from vitest
  collection.
```
